About

Claudia is a painter whose figurative works explore themes of human connection, nature, and femininity with sensitivity and depth.

Her practice is rooted in careful observation and a contemplative approach to color, texture, and form.

Each piece is created with intention, inviting viewers into a space of reflection.

Commissions

Claudia accepts a limited number of commissioned works each year.

Each painting is unique and created through a personalized process in close collaboration with the client.

Whether you envision a portrait, a figurative piece, or an exploration of a particular theme, she brings thoughtful attention to every commission.
